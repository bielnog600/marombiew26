- Plan translation (`planTranslation`, `planTranslationDisplay`, `supabase/functions/translate-plan`) is display-only and never blocks loading: the server extracts only displayed strings, caches pairs in plan_translations by content hash (publish trigger prewarms for students whose app_language is 'en'), and the app swaps text through those pairs while all logic reads the Portuguese original, so a slow AI cannot freeze screens or alter data.
- The student photo scanner reuses FoodScanner: pure `src/lib/foodPhotoScan.ts` owns lookup order, unit portions and meal-log rows; scanned items go into `meal_weigh_logs` with `origem='escaneado'` so day totals/rebalance reuse the weigh path.
- TABATA rules live in pure `tabataRules.ts`: the Ajuste Rápido day selector resolves the latest plan per weekday (load it / same plan / move-on-save) so a save never lands on another day's plan; the generator sends each day a quick-setup catalog (no bar/plate/smith/machine, no arm-support moves on upper-body days), then swaps slow-setup names deterministically and only warns about block variety, week repeats and the day's workout, so saved plans and the player format stay unchanged.
- TABATA warm-up and block intensity live in pure `tabataProgression.ts` and stay inside the markdown: Bloco 0 is a table under the Aquecimento section and intensity is a `**Intensidade:**` line, so old plans parse unchanged; the Player builds its steps through `tabataPlayerSteps` (legacy text lists convert on the fly), and the generator only normalizes the warm-up section (pairing one-side mobility into consecutive right/left rows whose side lives in the observation, never splitting a pair when trimming) and fills missing intensities deterministically; legacy text lists keep their own timing constant so saved plans play unchanged.
- The TABATA Player's screen rules (phase colours/intensity with a Moderado→Forte→Máximo fallback for blocks without intensity, phrases, header/round labels, total/remaining timeline, per-block progress, kcal, voice cue moments) live in pure `tabataPlayerView.ts`; the coach voice's moments, variation groups (encoded in the phrase key: the five legacy `tabata_*` keys plus `tabatav_<group>_<id>` rows the admin adds/removes), random no-repeat picks and spaced frequency live in pure `tabataVoiceCues.ts`, and clips play through `tabataVoicePlayer.ts`, which reuses the camera's `createCoachVoice`, only downloads approved TABATA system clips, never overlaps two clips and never falls back to device TTS (the existing beeps are the fallback), and the finished session (RPE 1–10) is one insert into `tabata_sessions` via `tabataSessionApi.ts`, so plans, steps and the camera voice stay unchanged.
- TABATA pause/resume rules (15 s auto-resume, 2 h warm-up warning, 24 h abandon, snapshot shape, local storage) live in pure `tabataResume.ts`; the Player stores one snapshot (exact plan markdown, step, remaining ms, pauses) both in localStorage and in `tabata_sessions.session_state.resume` (status in_progress/paused) through `tabataSessionApi.ts`, and the final RPE upserts the same row, so resume works across devices without changing the step engine, voices or the admin preview (which never persists).
- Cardio intervals, player and sessions stay in pure helpers: `cardioIntervals.ts` turns the coach's equipment fields into exact work/rest cycles (between Z1 warm-up and cool-down, idempotent) and is bundled into officeCore so cardio-agent, office-gerar and the Cardio IA screen normalize identically; `cardioPlayerView.ts` owns tones/tags, big params, "A seguir", segments, kcal (MET × weight × time) and the bike-position cue; bike positions from the equipment step are applied by `applyBikePositions` inside the same normalization; the cardio player is beeps-only (no recorded voice clips, which stay TABATA-only); `cardioResume.ts` reuses the TABATA resume rules; `cardioSessionApi.ts` upserts one `cardio_sessions` row per session id (progress → completed with finish details), so the plan JSON and parser stay unchanged.
- The student picks which cardio to do: pure `cardioChooser.ts` owns options (weekly protocols or the single protocol), short titles, chips, intensity bars, PICO/RECUP. boxes, grouped steps, the Mon–Sun week and the suggestion, matching finished `cardio_sessions` by `protocol_index` (same plan) and falling back to the title for older rows; the player only carries `protocolIndex` (and `autoStart`, which skips the idle screen straight into the prep countdown; admin preview keeps idle) through nav state, the resume snapshot and the saved row, so the step engine, resume and finish screen stay unchanged; `playerBeeper` keeps one app-wide audio context so `unlockPlayerAudio()` in the start tap still holds when the player mounts on iPhone.
